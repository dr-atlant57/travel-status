Bypassing Intermediary Friction Through CUG Networks

True optimization cannot be achieved by shifting from one public booking portal to another. Legal restrictions require hotels to list identical retail prices on all indexable websites. Bypassing these markups requires moving behind verification screens into a Closed User Group (CUG) layout, where raw net inventory is channeled straight to the user without storefront commissions.
